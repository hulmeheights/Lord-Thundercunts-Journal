# Lucid support answers

## 5 November 2026: trading at Lucid while automating elsewhere

**Where:** Lucid help chat, "Lucid AI" (Lucid's AI agent).
**Answer:** "Yes, that's generally fine. You can trade on our accounts while also trading elsewhere. Automated strategies and trade copiers are allowed on our side. The key rule is no hedging across firms: you cannot take opposite positions on the same or correlated contracts between different platforms. Also make sure your automated strategy is not high-frequency trading."
