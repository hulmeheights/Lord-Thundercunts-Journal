1. HOW THE PIECES FIT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
TradingView script  "MNQ Pass C - 4H bias + value area [1m]"
  - Finds the C trades and sends an alert for EVERY one of them, always.
  - It never stops sending. It does not know or care which of your
    accounts are eval or funded, passed, blown or paid out.
  - The chart (Strategy Tester) is only for looking at results and has
    no effect on the alerts.

Website (journal) + Mac bot
  - Each real account is toggled EVAL or FUNDED on the website.
  - On every alert, the website decides for EACH account whether it
    takes the trade, and with what size and target, from that
    account's own stage and numbers (section 5).
  - The Mac places the orders.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE TRADES (New York time, MNQ 1-minute chart)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Three possible trades per trading day, one per session:

  ASIA    Signal on the close of the 20:00 bar. Direction: LONG.
          No Asia trade on Friday or Saturday evenings.
          Time exit 02:59.
  LONDON  Note the open of the 03:01 bar. On the close of the 03:30 bar:
          price ABOVE that open -> SHORT, otherwise -> LONG
          (against the first 30 minutes). Time exit 09:39.
  NY      Note the open of the 09:41 bar. On the close of the 10:10 bar:
          price at or above that open -> LONG, otherwise -> SHORT
          (with the first 30 minutes). Time exit 16:09.

The trading day runs 18:00 to 17:00 New York, so the Asia evening
belongs to the next calendar day.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE TWO FILTERS (both must pass, or there is no trade and no alert)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
FILTER 1: 4H candle bias
  4H candles start 18:00, 22:00, 02:00, 06:00, 10:00, 14:00 New York.
  Bias = body of the last FINISHED 4H candle: close above open = UP,
  close below open = DOWN. Wicks are ignored.
    ASIA uses the 14:00-18:00 candle
    LONDON uses the 22:00-02:00 candle
    NY uses the 02:00-06:00 candle (not the 06:00-10:00 one)
  The trade direction must match the bias.

FILTER 2: outside the value area
  Volume profile of the day so far, from 20:00 New York up to the
  signal. 1-point bins; each bar's volume spread evenly from its low
  to its high. POC = busiest bin. Value area = grow out from the POC,
  adding the busier neighbouring bin each time, until 70% of volume
  is inside. VAH = top, VAL = bottom.
  ASIA uses the previous 24 hours (20:00 yesterday to 20:00 today).
  Price at the signal must be ABOVE VAH or BELOW VAL.

Do NOT wait for price to come back to the value area or POC, and do
NOT add extra entries later in the session. Both tested worse.

Orders fill at the open of the next bar.

5. WHAT THE WEBSITE DOES FOR EACH ACCOUNT ON EACH ALERT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The website keeps, per account: stage (EVAL / FUNDED / PRELIVE),
profit so far in this stage, highest end-of-day profit, best day,
today's profit, winning days ($150+), and whether it has a daily loss
limit.

STEP 1. Work out today's goal for the account.
  TOPSTEP EVAL:    goal = smaller of $1,640 and "still needed", where
                   still needed = larger of ($3,000 - profit) and
                   (best day / 0.55 - profit).
                   Worked example: fresh account, goal $1,640. If day 1
                   ends +$1,640, day 2's goal is $3,000 - $1,640 =
                   $1,360 (+$10 margin = about $1,370), and $1,640 is
                   then 54.7% of $3,010, inside the 55% rule. If a day
                   loses, the goal is again up to $1,640 the next day.
  TOPSTEP FUNDED:  goal = $1,640, until 5 winning days are banked;
                   after that, the smaller of $1,640 and
                   ($8,000 - profit).
                   (This replaced the earlier $750 risk / $2,500 day
                   cap for funded: the 12 Nov audit found $1,000 risk
                   with a $1,640 cap reaches the $8,000 payout more
                   often, 31% against 25.5%, and faster.)
  LUCID STEP 1:    goal = smaller of $1,200 and the larger of
                   ($3,000 - profit) and (best day / 0.40 - profit).
  LUCID PRE-LIVE:  goal = $2,000 - profit (no day cap).
  Left today = goal - today's profit.

STEP 2. Skip the account for this alert if:
  - left today is $0 or less (day goal reached), or
  - it already has an open position, or
  - its daily loss limit is used up, or
  - it is payout-ready (FUNDED: 5 winning days AND profit >= $8,000)
    -> stop trading it and request the payout, or
  - the account is passed/blown and waiting for you to change it.

STEP 3. Stop and size.
  Risk per trade: $1,000 in Topstep EVAL and FUNDED (50 points on
  10 MNQ), $1,000 in Lucid step 1, $1,250 in Lucid Pre-Live (62.5 pts).
  Room = profit - loss floor, where the loss floor trails the highest
  end-of-day profit by $2,000 and stops at 0 (= starting balance).
  With a daily loss limit, room = the smaller of that and
  ($1,000 + today's profit).
  Contracts = 10 if room >= risk, otherwise
              floor(room / (stop points x $2)) - never tighten the stop.
  If that is 0, the account cannot trade: in EVAL treat it as finished.

STEP 4. Target.
  Target points = (left today + $1 x contracts) / (contracts x $2),
  rounded up to the tick. If this trade would finish the account
  (pass or payout), aim for at least $25.

STEP 5. Exit.
  Bracket order: stop and target. If neither is hit, close at exit_by.

STEP 6. After the trade and at 18:00 each day, update the numbers.
  EVAL passes when profit >= $3,000 AND best day <= 55% of profit
  (Lucid: 40%). You then switch the account to FUNDED on the website.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ACCOUNT SETTINGS AND PAYOUTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Topstep: buy the 50K Combine with the daily loss limit added at
checkout (this doubles the payout cap to $4,000). $120 per eval.
Funded plan: run each account to an $8,000 balance with 5 winning
days, then request $4,000 (you receive $3,600). After a payout the
loss limit moves to $0 for good, so the remaining $4,000 is the buffer.

Being moved to Live: Topstep calls traders up at its discretion
(most between their 3rd and 5th payout). You cannot decline. All
Express Funded Accounts close when it happens, but a payout can be
requested from each one during the move. Check whether your bot's
API connection works on a Live account before relying on it there.

7. BACKTEST RESULTS (MNQ 1-minute data, Nov 2019 - Nov 2026)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Costs included: $1 per contract round trip, 1 tick slippage on stops,
a bar touching both stop and target counts as a loss.

EVAL (Topstep 55% rule)
  Pass rate 49% (2019-22: 48%, 2023-26: 50%), worst year 41% (2022)
  Profit factor 1.33, every year between 1.20 and 1.46
  Trade win rate 53%
  Median 7 trading days to pass; about 15-17 trading days per pass
  on one account (about 1.2 passes a month)
  Fees per pass about $245; most failed evals in a row: 6

TRADES
  About 0.7 a day, 3.5 a week (median 4). No trade on 43% of days.
  Longest run of days with no trade: 7.

FUNDED (Topstep, $1,000 risk, $1,640 day cap)
  Reach $4,000 balance + 5 winning days: 41% of funded accounts
  Reach $8,000 balance + 5 winning days: 30.5%
  Typical balance when the 5th winning day is banked: about $3,800

FIRST EVAL TO PAYOUT (fees at $120, averages)
  $4,000 funded -> $2,000 payout: about 5 evals, $610, about 3 months
  $8,000 funded -> $4,000 payout: about 7 evals, $840, about 4.5 months
  5 accounts copying, 5 x $4,000 payouts: about 35 evals, $4,190 on
  average; budget $9,000 for a bad run.

LUCIDMAXX (40% rule, then Pre-Live)
  Step 1 46%, Pre-Live 59%, both 27%; about 3.7 evals ($660 at $180)

8. HONEST LIMITS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Backtest only. Expect live pass rates a few points lower (43-47%).
- About 200 variations of C were tested on 12 Nov; none improved the
  eval pass rate and speed together. Speed comes from running more
  accounts at once, not from loosening the filters.
- Funded and payout figures rest on smaller samples than the eval
  figures.
- Not tested: trading on after the first payout, Lucid's live
  account, Lucid's Pre-Live scaling limits.
- Run on the practice account first.
